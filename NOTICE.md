- Insert series and nominal seat dimensions are based on CNC Kitchen's published dimensions, except the VORON seat diameter, which follows Voron Design's [Downdraft toolhead](https://github.com/VoronDesign/voron-downdraft) CAD.
- `assets/cnc-kitchen/dimensions-and-design-guidelines.png` is CNC Kitchen's Dimensions & Design Guidelines sheet, copied unchanged from its [store](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0654/4821/4767/files/Dimensions_and_Guidlines.png).
